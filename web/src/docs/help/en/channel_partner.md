
# Channel Partner Help Center

Welcome to the Partner Portal. Use this guide to navigate your dashboard.

## 1. Viewing Your Booked Policies
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > My Policies
**When to use:** To check the status of policies you have sourced.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "My Policies" in the navigation menu.
2. Here you can see all active and pending policies.
3. Use the search bar to find a specific policy number.

## 2. Checking Your Earnings
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Earnings
**When to use:** To see your commission and payouts.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to the Earnings tab.
2. View your total commission earned this month.
3. Check the breakdown by policy.

## 3. Raising a Query
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Support
**When to use:** When you need help from the agency.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on Support.
2. Submit your question or request.
3. The agency team will respond to you shortly.
