# Owner Help Center Documentation

## Dashboard
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard
**When to use:** Use the Dashboard to get a high-level overview of your entire business. It shows key metrics like total sales, active policies, upcoming renewals, and recent activities. It helps you quickly understand your business health without digging into detailed reports.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Log in to the application using your Owner credentials.
2. After logging in, you will be automatically redirected to the Dashboard.
3. Review the summary widgets for total leads, customers, policies, and revenue.
4. Click on any widget to drill down into the detailed list.

## Leads
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Leads
**When to use:** Use this feature to track potential customers who have shown interest but haven't bought a policy yet. You can add new leads, update their progress, and convert them to actual customers once they purchase.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "Leads" in the main navigation menu.
2. To add a new lead, click the "Add Lead" button at the top right.
3. Fill in their basic information (name, phone, interested product) and save.
4. To edit an existing lead, click on their name in the list.
5. Change the lead's status (e.g., "Contacted", "Follow-up") from the dropdown menu to track progress.
6. Once the lead agrees to buy, click the "Convert to Customer" button on their profile.

## Customers
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Customers
**When to use:** This section is used to manage all your active clients. You can view their contact details, history, and a list of all their active and expired policies.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to the "Customers" tab in the menu.
2. To add a new customer manually, click "Add Customer" and enter their details.
3. To view a customer's profile, click on their name.
4. On the customer profile page, you can see all their personal information, uploaded documents, and associated policies.
5. Use the "Edit" button to update their phone number or address if it changes.

## Policies
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Policies
**When to use:** Use this module to maintain a digital record of all insurance policies sold. It allows you to add new policies, upload official documents for safekeeping, and make changes or cancellations if needed.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to the "Policies" section.
2. Click "Add Policy" to enter a new policy record.
3. Select the customer, enter policy numbers, premium amounts, start and end dates.
4. Use the "Upload File" section to attach the scanned PDF copy of the policy document.
5. Save the policy.
6. To edit or cancel, open the policy from the list and use the "Edit" or "Cancel Policy" buttons.

## Renewals
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Renewals
**When to use:** This tool helps you retain existing business by tracking which policies are about to expire. You can view upcoming expiries and send timely reminders to customers so they renew with you.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "Renewals" in the menu.
2. The list will show policies expiring in the next 30, 60, or 90 days.
3. Filter the list by date range or policy type.
4. Select a customer and click "Send Reminder" to trigger an SMS or email notification.
5. Once the customer renews, you can click "Mark as Renewed" and link the new policy.

## Quote Requests
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Quote Requests
**When to use:** Use this when your Channel Partners submit requests for policy quotes on behalf of their clients. You can review their requests, respond with accurate pricing, and communicate via a built-in chat.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Open the "Quote Requests" module.
2. You will see a list of pending requests from your channel partners.
3. Click on a request to see the client's requirements.
4. Calculate the quote and enter the details in the response form.
5. Use the "Chat" section on the right to discuss terms or ask questions directly with the partner.
6. Click "Send Quote" to finalize your response.

## Claims
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Claims
**When to use:** Use the claims section to help your customers when they face an incident. You can log their claim details, track the process with the insurance company, and keep the customer updated on the status.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to the "Claims" section in the navigation.
2. Click "Log New Claim" when a customer reports an incident.
3. Select the relevant policy and enter the incident details (date, description, estimated loss).
4. Save the claim.
5. As the insurance company processes it, open the claim and update its status (e.g., "In Review", "Approved", "Rejected").

## Follow-ups & Tasks
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Tasks
**When to use:** This feature ensures nothing falls through the cracks. Use it to create to-do items for yourself or assign tasks to your employees, and monitor their completion.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to the "Tasks" section.
2. Click "Create Task".
3. Enter the task description, due date, and select an assignee (yourself or an employee).
4. You can optionally link the task to a specific lead or customer.
5. Click "Save".
6. The assignee will see the task in their list. Once they finish it, they can mark it as "Completed".
7. You can track all pending and completed tasks across the team.

## Finance Overview
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Finance > Overview
**When to use:** Access this to see the financial health of your agency. It provides a quick look at your income, expenses, profit/loss, and outstanding collections.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to "Finance", then click "Overview".
2. Review the summary charts showing monthly revenue and expenses.
3. Check the "Pending Collections" widget to see which customers or partners owe you money.
4. Look at the Profit/Loss summary for the current month or year.

## Finance Reports
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Finance > Reports
**When to use:** Use this feature when you need detailed financial data for accounting purposes or to share with your CA. You can generate comprehensive ledgers in Excel or PDF formats.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to "Finance" and select "Reports".
2. Choose the type of report you need (e.g., "Monthly Income", "Expense Statement").
3. Select the date range for the data.
4. Click "Generate".
5. Choose either "Export to Excel" or "Download PDF" to save the file to your computer.

## Finance Transactions
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Finance > Transactions
**When to use:** This is your digital ledger. Use it to manually record any money coming in (receipts) or going out (payments) that isn't automatically captured by the system.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to "Finance" and click "Transactions".
2. To record income, click "Add Receipt". For expenses, click "Add Payment".
3. Enter the amount, date, payment method (cash, bank transfer), and a description.
4. Link it to a specific customer, partner, or category if necessary.
5. Click "Save" to update your financial records.

## HR Attendance
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > HR > Attendance
**When to use:** Use this to monitor employee punctuality and presence. You can view the entire team's daily attendance, check the monthly grid, and approve any manual corrections requested by employees who forgot to clock in.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to "HR" and select "Attendance".
2. View the "Daily Log" to see who is present today and at what time they clocked in.
3. Switch to the "Monthly Grid" tab to see a calendar view for all employees.
4. Go to the "Correction Requests" tab to view employee requests for adjusting their time.
5. Click "Approve" or "Reject" on each request.

## HR Leave
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > HR > Leave
**When to use:** Manage your team's time off. Employees will submit leave requests here, and you can review them against their available balance before deciding to approve or reject.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to "HR" and click "Leave".
2. You will see a list of pending leave applications from employees.
3. Click on a request to view the dates, reason, and the employee's remaining leave balance.
4. Click the "Approve" or "Reject" button based on your decision.
5. The employee will be automatically notified of your choice.

## HR Payslips
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > HR > Payslips
**When to use:** At the end of the month, use this feature to calculate salaries based on attendance, leaves, and predefined salary structures, and then generate and distribute official payslips.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to "HR" and select "Payslips".
2. Select the month and year you are running payroll for.
3. Click "Generate Payroll". The system will automatically calculate base pay, deductions, and final amounts based on attendance.
4. Review the generated list to ensure accuracy.
5. Click "Publish Payslips" to make them visible to employees in their portals.

## People - Employees
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > People > Employees
**When to use:** Use this section to manage your staff. You can create accounts for new hires, assign them specific access permissions, or deactivate accounts when someone leaves the company.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to "People" and click "Employees".
2. To add a new staff member, click "Add Employee".
3. Fill in their details and set a default password.
4. Select their role (e.g., Sales, Support) which determines what they can see in the system.
5. Click "Save".
6. To remove access, click on an existing employee and select "Deactivate Account".

## People - Channel Partners
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > People > Channel Partners
**When to use:** Manage external agents who bring you business. Use this to onboard new partners and set their commission structures so payouts are calculated correctly.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to "People" and select "Channel Partners".
2. Click "Add Partner" to register a new agent.
3. Enter their contact details and business information.
4. Go to the "Commissions" tab on their profile to set their percentage cut for different policy types.
5. Save the profile. You can track their overall performance from this list.

## Settings
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Settings
**When to use:** This is the administrative control center. Use it to define company-wide rules, such as official working hours, penalties for arriving late, and the annual leave policy for employees.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on the gear icon or "Settings" at the bottom of the navigation menu.
2. Go to the "HR Settings" tab.
3. Under "Shift Timings", set the official start and end times for your office.
4. Under "Late Marks", define the grace period (e.g., 15 minutes) and how late arrivals affect attendance.
5. Under "Leave Policy", specify how many paid leaves an employee gets per year.
6. Click "Save Changes" to apply these rules globally.
