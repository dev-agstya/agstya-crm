# Employee Portal Guide

Welcome to the Employee Help Center! This guide covers all the actions you can perform in the system.

## Dashboard
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Home
**When to use:** Use this feature every time you log in to get a quick overview of your daily tasks, assigned targets, and pending follow-ups. It helps you prioritize your work for the day.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Log in to your account using your employee credentials.
2. You will automatically land on the Dashboard.
3. Review the "Targets" section to see your monthly or weekly goals.
4. Check the "Today's Tasks" section to see the immediate actions you need to take.
5. Click on any specific task or target to navigate directly to its details.

## Leads & Customers
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Leads & Customers
**When to use:** Use this when you need to view the list of leads or customers assigned to you, update their contact information, or record the outcome of a recent interaction.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "Leads & Customers" in the main navigation menu.
2. Select either "My Leads" or "My Customers" depending on who you want to manage.
3. Use the search bar or filters to find a specific person.
4. Click on the name of the lead or customer to open their detailed profile.
5. Update their details, add notes from your recent conversation, and click "Save Changes".

## Policies & Renewals
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Policies
**When to use:** Use this to manage active insurance policies assigned to you, check coverage details, or track upcoming renewals to ensure continuous coverage for your clients.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to the "Policies" section from the left menu.
2. Go to the "Upcoming Renewals" tab to see which policies are expiring soon.
3. Click on a specific policy number to view the policyholder's details, premium amount, and due dates.
4. Contact the customer to remind them about the renewal.
5. Once renewed or if they decline, update the policy status accordingly and add a note.

## Quote Requests & Claims
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Quotes & Claims
**When to use:** Use this feature if you are assigned to the quote generation or claim processing desk. It allows you to process incoming requests from customers seeking new pricing or filing a claim.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "Quotes & Claims" in the navigation bar.
2. Select either "New Quote Requests" or "Pending Claims".
3. Choose a request from the list to view the submitted documents and details.
4. Review the information carefully. If any document is missing, click "Request Information" to notify the customer.
5. If everything is complete, process the quote or claim and change the status to "Approved" or "Processed".

## Follow-ups & Tasks
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Tasks
**When to use:** Use this to keep track of reminders, scheduled callbacks, and manual tasks assigned to you by your manager.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Open the "Tasks" menu.
2. View your list of pending follow-ups organized by due date and priority.
3. Click on a task to read the instructions or the context of the follow-up.
4. Perform the required action (e.g., calling a customer).
5. Once done, mark the task as "Completed" and write a brief summary of the outcome.

## HR Attendance
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > HR > Attendance
**When to use:** Use this daily to log your working hours. You can also use it to review your monthly attendance grid or request a correction if you forgot to punch in or out.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to "HR" and click on "Attendance".
2. At the start of your shift, click the large "Punch In" button.
3. At the end of your shift, click "Punch Out".
4. To view your history, scroll down to the "Monthly Grid" to see your daily logs.
5. If you missed a punch, click "Request Correction" next to the specific date, enter the correct time, provide a reason, and submit it for your manager's approval.

## HR Leave
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > HR > Leave
**When to use:** Use this when you need to take time off for vacation, sickness, or personal reasons. You can also use it to check how many leave days you have remaining for the year.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to "HR" and select "Leave".
2. Check the top section to view your current leave balance for different categories (e.g., Sick Leave, Casual Leave).
3. To request time off, click "Apply for Leave".
4. Select the leave type, choose the start and end dates, and provide a brief reason.
5. Click "Submit Application" and wait for your manager to approve it.

## HR Payslips
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > HR > Payslips
**When to use:** Use this to view your salary details, tax deductions, and download your official monthly payslips for personal records or financial applications.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to "HR" and click on "Payslips".
2. You will see a list of available payslips organized by month and year.
3. Locate the month you want to view.
4. Click "View" to see the breakdown of your earnings and deductions on the screen.
5. Click the "Download PDF" button to save a copy to your computer.
