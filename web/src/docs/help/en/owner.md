
# Owner Help Center

Welcome to the Owner Help Center. As an owner, you have full control over the CRM.

## 1. Managing Employees
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > People > Employees > Add Employee
**When to use:** When a new staff member joins your agency.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to the Employees section from the left sidebar.
2. Click on "Add Employee" in the top right.
3. Fill in the employee's basic details (Name, Email, Role).
4. Assign permissions based on what they need to do.
5. Click "Save". The employee will receive an invite email.

## 2. Managing Finances
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Finance > Bank Accounts
**When to use:** To track incoming payments and outgoing commissions.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to Finance.
2. Add your agency's bank accounts.
3. You can upload bank statements and reconcile payments against policies.

## 3. Reports & Analytics
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Reports
**When to use:** To see overall business health and performance.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Open the Reports tab.
2. Select the date range you wish to analyze.
3. Review total premium collected, active policies, and employee targets.
