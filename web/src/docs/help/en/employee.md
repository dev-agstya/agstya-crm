
# Employee Help Center

Welcome to the Employee Help Center. Here is how you can manage your day-to-day tasks.

## 1. Adding a Customer
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Customers > Add Customer
**When to use:** When you need to create a profile for a new lead or client.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to Customers in the sidebar.
2. Click "Add Customer".
3. Enter their personal information, phone number, and address.
4. Save the customer record.

## 2. Creating a Policy
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Policies > Add Policy
**When to use:** When a customer successfully purchases an insurance policy.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to Policies.
2. Select the Customer you just added.
3. Fill in the policy details (Insurer, Policy Type, Premium Amount).
4. Upload any required documents.
5. Save the policy.

## 3. Claim Processing
**Breadcrumb:** Dashboard > Claims > File Claim
**When to use:** When a customer needs to file an insurance claim.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to the Claims section.
2. Search for the customer's policy.
3. Click "File Claim" and enter the incident details.
4. Update the claim status as you receive updates from the insurer.
