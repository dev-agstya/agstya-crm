# Channel Partner Help Center

Welcome to the Channel Partner Help Center! As a Channel Partner, you play a vital role in our business by bringing in new customers and managing their insurance needs. This guide will walk you through all the features available to you in the Partner Portal.

## Dashboard
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > Dashboard
**When to use:** Use the Dashboard to get a quick overview of your entire business performance. It gives you a snapshot of your recent sales, pending quotes, upcoming renewals, and overall commission earnings at a single glance. It's the first thing you see when you log in and helps you decide what to focus on for the day.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Log in to your Partner Portal using your registered email and password.
2. The first screen you land on is the Dashboard.
3. Review the summary cards at the top to see your total policies sold, total premium collected, and total commission earned this month.
4. Scroll down to view charts and graphs that show your performance trends over the last few months.
5. Check the "Recent Activity" section to see the latest updates on your quote requests or claim statuses.

## KYC & Profile
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > KYC & Profile
**When to use:** Use this section to update your personal and professional information, upload necessary identification documents like PAN and Aadhar cards, and add your bank account details. This is absolutely essential to ensure you receive your commission payouts smoothly and on time.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "KYC & Profile" in the main navigation menu on the left side of your screen.
2. Under the "Personal Information" tab, ensure your name, contact number, and email address are correct.
3. Switch to the "Documents" tab to upload scanned copies or clear photos of your PAN Card and Aadhar Card. Click the "Upload" button next to each document type.
4. Go to the "Bank Details" tab to enter your Account Number, IFSC Code, and Bank Name. This is where your payouts will be credited.
5. Click the "Save Changes" or "Submit for Verification" button at the bottom of the page to save your details.

## Customers
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > Customers
**When to use:** Use this feature when you need to maintain a record of the people you are selling policies to. By adding your customers here, you can easily select them later when requesting a quote or issuing a policy, saving you time from typing their details repeatedly.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to the "Customers" section from the left-hand menu.
2. Click on the "Add New Customer" button located at the top right corner.
3. Fill in the customer's personal details, including their full name, mobile number, email address, and residential address.
4. If the customer represents a business, fill in the company name and GST details in the provided fields.
5. Review the information to ensure there are no spelling mistakes.
6. Click "Save Customer" to add them to your customer directory.

## Quote Requests
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > Quote Requests
**When to use:** Use this feature when a customer wants to buy a new policy or switch to a different insurance plan, and you need a price estimate from the agency. You can upload their Registration Certificate (RC) or previous policy documents here, and chat directly with our quote desk team to negotiate or ask questions.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "Quote Requests" in the main menu.
2. Click the "Request New Quote" button.
3. Select the customer from your drop-down list (or add a new one if you haven't already).
4. Choose the type of insurance they need (e.g., Motor, Health, Life).
5. In the "Upload Documents" section, attach clear photos or PDFs of their RC copy and previous year's insurance policy, if applicable.
6. Add any specific requirements or notes in the comments box.
7. Click "Submit Request".
8. To follow up, click on a pending quote in your list. You can use the chat box on the right side of the screen to talk directly to the quote desk agent handling your request.

## Policies
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > Policies
**When to use:** Use this section to view a complete list of all the insurance policies you have successfully sold. You can use this to download policy PDF documents to send to your customers, or just to keep track of your successful sales.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Go to the "Policies" section using the left-hand menu.
2. You will see a list of all active and expired policies you have sold.
3. Use the search bar at the top to find a specific policy by searching for the customer's name, vehicle number, or policy number.
4. Click on the "View" button next to a policy to see all its details, including premium amount and coverage dates.
5. To download a copy of the policy document, click the "Download PDF" button. You can then print it or email it directly to your customer.

## Renewals
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > Renewals
**When to use:** Use this feature to track which of your customers' policies are about to expire. This is your most important tool for retaining customers and earning recurring commissions. It helps you know exactly who to call and when.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "Renewals" in the navigation menu.
2. By default, you will see a list of policies expiring in the next 30 days.
3. You can change the filter at the top to see policies expiring in 7 days, 60 days, or a custom date range.
4. Review the list to identify which customers need to be contacted for renewal.
5. Once you have spoken to a customer, you can click on the renewal entry to add notes about your conversation or schedule a follow-up reminder.
6. Click "Request Renewal Quote" directly from this screen to start the process of getting them a price for the next year.

## Claims
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > Claims
**When to use:** Use this feature when one of your customers has met with an accident or needs to file a claim, and they ask for your help. Logging the claim here ensures our claims team starts working on it immediately and allows you to track its progress.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Navigate to the "Claims" section from the left-hand menu.
2. Click the "Log New Claim" button.
3. Search for and select the relevant policy from the list.
4. Fill out the claim intimation form with the date, time, and a brief description of the incident as reported by your customer.
5. Upload any immediate evidence provided by the customer, such as photos of the damage or a copy of the FIR (if applicable).
6. Click "Submit Claim".
7. You can check back on this screen anytime to see if the claim is "Pending", "Approved", or "Settled".

## Ledger / Payouts
**Breadcrumb:** Partner Portal > Ledger / Payouts
**When to use:** Use this section to view your financial transactions with the agency. This is where you can track how much commission you have earned for each policy, view any deductions, and see a record of all the money that has been transferred to your bank account.
**Step-by-step:**
1. Click on "Ledger / Payouts" in the main menu.
2. The "Commission Ledger" tab shows a detailed, line-by-line breakdown of commissions earned for every single policy you have sold.
3. The "Payouts" tab shows a history of all the consolidated payments sent to your bank account.
4. Click on any specific payout row to see a detailed statement showing exactly which policies made up that total payment.
5. If you notice any discrepancies, use the "Raise Query" button next to a transaction to contact the accounts department.
